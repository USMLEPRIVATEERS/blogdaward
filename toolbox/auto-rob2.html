Domain 2: Select Effect of Interest

Choose whether you are assessing the effect of assignment to intervention (intention-to-treat) or the effect of adhering to intervention (per-protocol).

Effect of Assignment

Intention-to-treat (ITT) effect. Assesses what happens when participants are assigned to intervention, regardless of whether they adhered to it.

Effect of Adhering

Per-protocol effect. Assesses what happens when participants actually adhere to the intervention as intended.
